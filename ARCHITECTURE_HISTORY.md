# M1 architecture history and origin audit

**Public technical derivative, 24 October 2026.** Research archived; M1
**NOT_FROZEN**. This account summarizes a local source audit without publishing
private conversations or personal session logs. It makes no new architecture
decision and does not change historical contracts or results.

**Main finding:** the original current-turn M1 was a general distributed semantic
state. TYPE7 event structure arose as the bounded experimental ontology of E2G
and subsequently became central to the normative M1 completion contract. The
universal necessity of TYPE7 as the exclusive content interface for the entire
larger system was not established by the audited evidence. Event structures
themselves were not refuted.

## What is documented

1. **13 August: initial distributed state.** ADR0005 and the first M1
   preregistration specify a replaceable text-only donor boundary, a canonical
   language latent, and a learned 128-dimensional current-turn semantic state.
   This initial experiment was also bounded: MASSIVE intent-family fixtures and
   explicit semantic contrasts, not a qualified universal representation.
2. **20 August: useful similarities were insufficient.** E1 ready encoders passed
   ordinary semantic/consumer checks but failed mandatory critical contrasts.
   E2 then compared content-only, relational and combined representations. The
   measured implementations did not close the critical/OOD gates; that did not
   disprove distributed or hybrid representations as a class.
3. **E2R/E2F: missing variables in limited controls.** Global role/filler controls
   preserved some participant identity but did not represent all required
   predicate identity, event ownership and event-order information. Controls
   that were not run after an earlier gate failed remain NOT_RUN.
4. **E2G: bounded oracle sufficiency.** Gold predicates, event-local arguments,
   polarity and directed event relations supported the frozen E2 consumer task.
   The representation had zero learned parameters and received Gold primitive
   factors. Its passing results establish sufficiency for that controlled task,
   not raw-text extraction, universality, minimality or a unique architecture.
5. **28 August and 5 September: engineering decomposition.** Scope-v2 assigned
   five factors: LOC, TYPE, BIND, POL and REL. CAN supports identity/encoding;
   it is not a sixth official semantic factor. The completion contract then made
   learned text extraction, factor gates, integration and independent final
   evidence forward engineering obligations.
6. **Later work: development success did not ensure generalization.** The sixth
   final followed DEV 296/296 PASS but returned 215 PASS /73 FAIL /8 NOT_RUN.
   Subsequent support, conditioning and scorer experiments remained bounded
   development hypotheses. The last closed LOC support-only experiment improved
   newly supplied support but failed broad retention/transfer; later MLP pilots
   did not close the task either.

## Why seven types appeared

The seven types corresponded to existing controlled E2 task families:
PURCHASE for content/lexical traps, GIVE and SHOW for role and relation identity,
OWN for possession/nested composition, OPEN/CLOSE for polarity and event order,
and REPORT for source/addressee composition. An early oracle contract explicitly
omitted a general content channel for that task because the purchase predicate
and its agent/object determined the frozen content target. That experimental
choice is not a demonstration that these seven types exhaust human meaning.

The historical audit also recovered a design sketch with both `content_latent`
and `event_structure`. The general content channel was optional in the E2G
oracle exercise. No separately accepted ADR conclusively removing all such
content from the future system was found in the audited source set. The later
completion contract still governs what the bounded M1 qualification required;
the audit did not silently weaken it.

## What remains unknown

- A documented scientific or downstream-system necessity for TYPE7 as the one
  exclusive universal semantic boundary was not found.
- Personal attribution of early assistant proposals to a specific model named
  Astra was not established. Available conversational roles alone cannot prove
  model identity or authorship.
- A qualified open-ontology expansion protocol and complete final M1→M2/M4
  downstream content contract were not established by this audit.
- The audit was limited to accessible documents, two local historical snapshot
  commits, selected archives and discussion exports. Absence of a finding is
  limited to that search; it is not proof that no other record ever existed.

## Important interface limitation

The event bank represents supported events, arguments, polarity and relations.
An empty asserted-event set is not an empty meaning. If two semantically distinct
inputs both correctly produce E0 and a consumer sees only that bank, the bank
cannot convey distinctions absent from it. This is a conditional information
argument, not a new inference experiment or proof that the actual runtime always
uses a bank-only boundary. Existing runtime objects retain raw text/features;
their presence does not establish a qualified general semantic channel.

## Reusable engineering value

Preserve minimal semantic contrasts, event-local ownership, factor-specific
diagnostics, causal/consumer evidence, versioned interfaces, restoration checks,
negative results and numerical provenance. Their value does not require claiming
that TYPE7 is universal. A later Collab architecture must establish its own
functional claims; this archival publication implements no successor.
