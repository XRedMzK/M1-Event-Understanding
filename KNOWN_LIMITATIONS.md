# Known limitations at research closure

**M1 NOT_FROZEN; research branch archived on 24 October 2026.** Issues describe
unresolved research problems, not promised fixes or evidence that gates passed.

## LOC

The principal late failure is assertion/span scoring and generalization, with
different residual regimes for different checkpoints. On frozen 4abd mixed672
and Composition112, missed Gold candidates exist and forced-WITH solutions are
legal; the native DP exactly reproduces the saved optimum. Negative EVENT−NULL
scores dominate. Some further misses occur after TYPE-log-probability coupling.
This is not evidence of an interval-search bug or a universal feature ceiling.

Adding mixed support improved its own TRAIN population but harmed retention and
some previously passing DEV gates. A constant global or per-type gain bias cannot
recover every exact Gold set in the checked populations; that result does not
prove the 95% gates are impossible. The nonlinear pilot's falling NLL did not
produce adequate whole-TRAIN decisions. Its interrupted continuation has no
closed quality result.

The controlled transfer sets are authored development populations with disclosed
shared ontology, instructions, lexical/HEAD overlap and factor confounds. Consumed
Composition112 and fresh280 are not independent final qualifications after
inspection. Current complete three-origin qualification is unavailable.

## TYPE, BIND and downstream factors

- Pure frozen TYPE on fixed boundaries and actual jointly scored TYPE are
  distinct. Conditional successes do not establish actual TYPE compatibility=1.0.
- BIND has its own Gold-event errors. On old FZ0 DEV256, widening K=8/16/32
  changed Gold survival but not top-1: 0/490 focused and 0/485 original erroneous
  events were classified as search errors. These percentages cannot be assigned
  to newer checkpoints without measuring them.
- Decoder score `S_dec` sums conditional log-softmax terms; raw energy `J_raw`
  omits prefix-dependent normalizers. They are different objectives. Old rank
  reversals affected 19/490 and 22/485 erroneous events, not all binding failures.
- The often quoted 40% LOC→BIND dependence is missing coverage: in DEV368,
  698/1720 positive role slots (40.58%) belong to 266 missing Gold event boundaries.
  It is not a measured promise of a 40-point BIND gain from Gold localization.
  A matched Gold-HEAD intervention on the same weights/data/evaluator was absent.
- Historical POL component qualification remains valid only in its stated scope.
  Predicted-HEAD and changed transfer contexts require their own qualification.
  REL and full consumer integration remain unresolved.
- CAN is a learned finite-inventory identity mechanism. A decoded name, catalogue
  membership, or exact Gold-mention score is not universal entity semantics or
  physical-instance coreference.

## Runtime, portability and reproduction

The ordinary repository runtime preserves the Experimental 1 tuple. It does not
silently adopt later research weights. Historical scripts in the research asset
use different architectures, imports, strict custody manifests and run paths;
they are archival source, not an installed `m1` API or a one-command retraining
package. Missing RAW banks, omitted detailed payloads and redacted paths are
listed explicitly. A hash alone does not make missing bytes available.

[Windows PR #5](https://github.com/XRedMzK/M1-Event-Understanding/pull/5) remains
separate. Its proposed blanket Windows bypass of native factor bit checks has
not been adopted by this closure. Its symlink-test change is a different concern.
The submitter's Windows results are not independently reproduced here. Native
numerical parity across hardware/batching is not promised by this archive.

Frozen foreign donor weights/tokenizers, dependency wheels, private chat exports,
secret qualification seed files and raw operational host journals are excluded.
Research source keeps historical proposals and statuses; those do not authorize
restarting training after the closure decision.
